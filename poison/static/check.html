Check before you send

Scammers plant addresses that share the first and last characters of one you have paid, hoping you copy the fake one from your history. Paste the address you are about to send to.

Whatever the result

Get the address from the recipient directly, not from your transaction history. Compare the whole address, not only the start and end. For a large payment, send a small test first.
